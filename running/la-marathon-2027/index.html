Strides

After the run · flat ground

About 20 seconds of quick, relaxed running: fast but not a sprint, tall posture, quick light feet. Walk all the way back between each one. The number is how many. They improve running economy and leg turnover at almost no cost to recovery.

Calf hold

Primer · tendon stiffness

Both feet on the ToePro, toes up. Rise halfway and hold dead still for the count. Hard, but pain ≤3/10. Before every run, and the only foot work on long-run Fridays.

Heel raise

Achilles + plantar fascia · Monday

One leg on the ToePro, toes higher than the heel, dumbbell in the hand on that side, other hand on a wall. 3 s up over the big toe, 1 s hold, 3 s down until the heel is below the ramp. Knee straight. Reps are per leg.

Bent-knee raise

Soleus · Wednesday

The heel raise with the knee bent ~30° the whole time, one leg, dumbbell in hand. Same 3 s up · 1 s · 3 s down. The bent knee moves the work to the soleus, the calf muscle that tires late in a marathon and guards the Achilles downhill.

Band inversion

Posterior tibialis · Monday

Seated, leg out, band round the forefoot pulling it outward. Heel still, turn the sole in and down (2 s), back slowly (3 s). Keeps the arch up late in a race. 11/10/10 = reps per set.

Pogos

Tendon as a spring · Wednesday

Small, fast hops on the spot off the balls of both feet: stiff ankles, almost no knee bend, quick contacts, like skipping rope. The number is total contacts. New for you: they start small in wk 5.
